01 · Know the exam

IELTS at a glance

Use the test structure as your study map. Your preparation should train both English ability and task-specific technique.

🎧

Listening

Four recorded sections with a range of everyday and academic situations. Train prediction, spelling, signpost words and paraphrase recognition.

📖

Reading

Work through passages and question types such as headings, matching, multiple choice, completion and True/False/Not Given.

✍️

Writing

Task 1 focuses on presenting information; Task 2 asks you to develop a clear, relevant response to an argument, problem or question.

🗣️

Speaking

A face-to-face assessment covering personal questions, a longer individual turn and deeper discussion. Fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation all matter.

02 · Listening

Hear the idea, not just the word

IELTS frequently changes the wording between the question and the recording. Strong listeners expect the same meaning to arrive in different language.

  • Preview questions and predict the kind of answer required.
  • Underline key words, but listen for meaning rather than exact repetition.
  • Watch for corrections, contrasts, numbers, dates and spelling.
  • Practise with transcripts after the first attempt to identify missed paraphrases.
  • Record recurring spelling and vocabulary errors in an error log.

Paraphrase trainer

obtain→get / receive
scarce→limited / hard to find
familiar with→accustomed to
over many years→over a long period

Reading decision rule

Question → locate → compare meaning → decide.

Don't match words.Match the claim, relationship and evidence.
Don't assume.If the passage does not support it, treat it as unsupported.

03 · Reading

Read strategically

Reading success is not simply reading faster. It is finding the right part of the text and checking whether it really answers the question.

04 · Writing

Plan first. Then write precisely.

Good IELTS writing is controlled: answer the exact question, organise ideas logically, support them, and use accurate language.

TASK 1 · ACADEMIC

Describe visual information

Identify the main features, select meaningful comparisons and support them with accurate figures. Avoid listing every detail.

Introduction → Overview → Details 1 → Details 2
TASK 1 · GENERAL TRAINING

Write the right letter

Identify the situation, relationship and purpose. Keep the tone appropriate and make every bullet point clear.

Purpose → Bullet 1 → Bullet 2 → Bullet 3
TASK 2

Answer the argument

Determine the essay type before planning. Make your position clear when required and make each body paragraph do one clear job.

Position → Idea → Explain → Example → Link

Task 2 question families

OpinionDiscussionAdvantages / disadvantagesProblems / solutionsDirect questions
Common score killers: answering a different question, unclear position, weak development, memorised language that does not fit, poor paragraph control, inaccurate grammar, and irrelevant detail.

05 · Speaking

Sound natural, not rehearsed

The examiner is assessing your English communication, not whether your personal story is impressive. Give a direct answer, then extend it naturally.

Part 1Short questions about familiar subjects. Answer + reason + small detail.
Part 2Prepare quickly, then speak continuously around the cue-card points.
Part 3Discuss broader ideas. Compare, explain causes, evaluate and predict.

Easy extension pattern

Answer → Why? → Example → Contrast → Future

Example: “Yes, I do. Mainly because… For example… However… In the future, I’d like to…”
