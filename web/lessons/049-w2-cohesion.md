# BUYE IELTS Module 049: Cohesion Without Over-Linking

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Use logical relationships naturally.

## Lesson
Coherence comes from clear ideas and referencing, not from inserting connectors into every sentence.

## Practice
Replace repeated “Moreover” with a natural continuation.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Any accurate alternative or no connector where unnecessary.
