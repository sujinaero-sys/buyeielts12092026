# BUYE IELTS Module 018: Follow Cause and Effect

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Track relationships, not just keywords.

## Lesson
Listen for because, therefore, leads to, results in and consequently. These words connect facts.

## Practice
Identify the effect: “Heavy rain caused the river to overflow.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
river overflow
