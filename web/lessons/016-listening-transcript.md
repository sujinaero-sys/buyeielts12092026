# BUYE IELTS Module 016: Use the Transcript as a Diagnostic Tool

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Turn mistakes into targeted practice.

## Lesson
After an attempt, compare the recording transcript with your prediction. Label the failure: vocabulary, attention, paraphrase, spelling or tracking.

## Practice
Label this error: you knew the word but missed it because the speaker corrected the first answer.

## Suggested answer / rationale
attention/distractor
