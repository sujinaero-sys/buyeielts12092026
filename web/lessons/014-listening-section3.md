# BUYE IELTS Module 014: Section 3: Academic Discussion

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Track agreement and disagreement.

## Lesson
Listen for soft disagreement: I see your point, but…; I'm not convinced…; another possibility is…

## Practice
Rewrite “I disagree” as a softer academic discussion phrase.

## Suggested answer / rationale
I'm not convinced by that view.
