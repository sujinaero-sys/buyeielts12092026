# BUYE IELTS Module 060: Discuss Government Responsibility

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Define what government can realistically do.

## Lesson
Policies should be connected to incentives, infrastructure, regulation or education.

## Practice
Propose one government response to food waste.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Collection incentives/standards.
