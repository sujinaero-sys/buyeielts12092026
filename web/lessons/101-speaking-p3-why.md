# BUYE IELTS Module 101: Part 3: Explain Why

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Move from opinion to cause.

## Lesson
Use because, partly because, due to and this means that.

## Practice
Why do people move to cities?

## Suggested answer / rationale
Cause + explanation.
