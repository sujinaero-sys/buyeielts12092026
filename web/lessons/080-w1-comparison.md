# BUYE IELTS Module 080: Comparison Language

**Track:** Writing Task 1  
**Goal:** Make relationships explicit.

## Lesson
Use compared with, whereas, while, respectively, higher than and similar to accurately.

## Practice
Combine two sentences about two categories.

## Suggested answer / rationale
One accurate comparison sentence.
