# BUYE IELTS Module 025: Matching Headings

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Match the central idea of a paragraph.

## Lesson
Ignore attractive details. Ask what the paragraph is mainly doing or arguing.

## Practice
Paragraph discusses causes, then explains one example. Choose a heading about causes rather than the example.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Causes heading
