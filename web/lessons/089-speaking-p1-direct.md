# BUYE IELTS Module 089: Part 1: Answer Directly

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Give a natural first response.

## Lesson
Start with a direct answer, then add a reason or detail. Avoid memorised mini-speeches.

## Practice
Question: Do you enjoy cooking? Give a two-sentence answer.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Any direct answer + reason/detail.
