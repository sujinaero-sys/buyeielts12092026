# BUYE IELTS Module 017: Paraphrase Radar

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Recognise equivalent meaning.

## Lesson
Ielts questions often use one expression while the recording uses another. Train meaning pairs, not isolated synonyms.

## Practice
Question: “The course is compulsory.” Recording: “Students are required to take it.” Do they match?

## Suggested answer / rationale
Yes
