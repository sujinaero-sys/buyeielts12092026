# BUYE IELTS Module 021: Skim for Structure

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Find the map of a passage before detail reading.

## Lesson
Read the title, opening lines and paragraph beginnings. Identify topic progression rather than trying to understand every sentence.

## Practice
Give each paragraph a 3–5 word label.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Any accurate short label.
