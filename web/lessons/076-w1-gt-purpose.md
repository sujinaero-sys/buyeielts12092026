# BUYE IELTS Module 076: General Training Letter Purpose

**Track:** Writing Task 1  
**Goal:** Make the purpose clear.

## Lesson
Identify why you are writing before choosing tone and details.

## Practice
Write the purpose for requesting a schedule change.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Request a change and explain the reason.
