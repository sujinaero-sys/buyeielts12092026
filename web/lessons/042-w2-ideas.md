# BUYE IELTS Module 042: Generate Two Strong Ideas

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Prefer developable ideas to long lists.

## Lesson
Choose ideas that you can explain, illustrate and connect to the question.

## Practice
Generate two reasons for improving public transport.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Affordability/access; congestion/environment.
