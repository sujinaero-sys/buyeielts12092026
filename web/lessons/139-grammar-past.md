# BUYE IELTS Module 139: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Separate finished past time from experience/change to now.

## Lesson
Past simple usually has a finished time; present perfect connects past experience or change to the present.

## Practice
“I visited Delhi in 2024.” vs “I have visited Delhi.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
Past simple has finished time; present perfect has present relevance.
