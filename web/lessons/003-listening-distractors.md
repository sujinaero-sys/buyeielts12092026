# BUYE IELTS Module 003: Spot Corrections and Distractors

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Avoid answers that are mentioned but rejected.

## Lesson
Speakers may introduce one detail and then correct it. The final confirmed information is what matters.

## Practice
Which answer should you keep? “The meeting was planned for Tuesday—actually, Wednesday afternoon is better.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
Wednesday afternoon
