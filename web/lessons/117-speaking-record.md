# BUYE IELTS Module 117: Self-Recording Review

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Use recordings to improve.

## Lesson
Listen once for fluency, once for vocabulary, once for grammar/pronunciation.

## Practice
Record a one-minute answer and write three observations.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Three specific observations.
