# BUYE IELTS Module 143: Countable and Uncountable Nouns

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Avoid common noun errors.

## Lesson
Information, advice, equipment and research are commonly uncountable.

## Practice
Correct: “many informations”.

## Suggested answer / rationale
much information / pieces of information.
