# BUYE IELTS Module 149: Passive Voice

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Focus on process or action rather than actor.

## Lesson
Useful when the actor is unknown, obvious or less important.

## Practice
Change “Researchers collected the data” to passive.

## Suggested answer / rationale
The data were collected by researchers.
