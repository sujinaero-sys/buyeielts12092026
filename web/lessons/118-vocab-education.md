# BUYE IELTS Module 118: Education Vocabulary

**Track:** Vocabulary  
**Goal:** Build usable words for study and policy.

## Lesson
access, curriculum, assessment, tuition, vocational training, literacy. Learn collocations, not isolated words.

## Practice
Write one speaking and one writing sentence with “vocational training”.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Two accurate contextual sentences.
