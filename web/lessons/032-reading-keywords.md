# BUYE IELTS Module 032: Keyword Quality

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Choose search terms that are distinctive.

## Lesson
Names, numbers and unusual concepts are usually better scanning anchors than common words.

## Practice
Choose the better scan anchor: “important” or “1987 prototype reactor”.
