# BUYE IELTS Module 019: Predict Word Forms

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Use grammar to narrow possibilities.

## Lesson
A gap after an article often needs a noun; after 'to' it may need a base verb; after 'very' an adjective/adverb may fit.

## Practice
Predict the form: “The project was highly ____.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
successful / effective
