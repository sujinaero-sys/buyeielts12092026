# BUYE IELTS Module 111: Pronunciation: Word Stress

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Make important words clear.

## Lesson
Incorrect stress can make familiar words harder to understand.

## Practice
Mark stress in: development, economic, photography.

## Suggested answer / rationale
deVELOPment; ecoNOMic; phoTOGraphy.
