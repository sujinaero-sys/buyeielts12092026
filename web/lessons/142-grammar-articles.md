# BUYE IELTS Module 142: Articles

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Control a/an/the and zero article.

## Lesson
Use articles according to specificity, countability and whether a noun is general or specific.

## Practice
Correct: “Government should provide the solution.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
“The government should provide a solution.” or context-dependent alternatives.
