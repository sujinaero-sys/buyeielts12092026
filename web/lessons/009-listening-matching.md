# BUYE IELTS Module 009: Matching Speakers to Ideas

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Track opinions across several voices.

## Lesson
Before listening, distinguish the people or categories. After each opinion, mentally attach it to the correct label.

## Practice
Speaker A supports online study; Speaker B prefers classroom interaction. Match each view to its speaker.

## Suggested answer / rationale
A—online study; B—classroom interaction
