# BUYE IELTS Module 157: Grammar Range Strategy

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Use variety without forcing complexity.

## Lesson
Mix accurate simple, compound and complex sentences. Complexity is useful only when it communicates a relationship.

## Practice
Rewrite two short sentences as one accurate complex sentence.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Any accurate combination.
