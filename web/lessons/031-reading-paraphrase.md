# BUYE IELTS Module 031: Reading Paraphrase Radar

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Recognise changed wording.

## Lesson
Compare meaning, not surface words. Look for word-family changes, active/passive shifts and noun/verb substitutions.

## Practice
“reduce costs” and “lower expenditure” express the same idea.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Yes
