# BUYE IELTS Module 108: Fluency: Keep Moving

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Reduce long silent gaps.

## Lesson
Use natural planning phrases and paraphrase when a word is missing.

## Practice
Replace silence with a short thinking phrase.

## Suggested answer / rationale
“Let me think about that…”
