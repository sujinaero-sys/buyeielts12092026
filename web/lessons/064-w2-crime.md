# BUYE IELTS Module 064: Crime Essay Toolkit

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Distinguish prevention from punishment.

## Lesson
Discuss incentives, rehabilitation, enforcement and social causes.

## Practice
Give one reason rehabilitation can reduce reoffending.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Addresses skills and behaviour after release.
