# BUYE IELTS Module 001: Predict the Answer Before You Listen

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Predict answer type from grammar and context.

## Lesson
Read the sentence around the blank. Decide whether you need a name, place, number, noun, adjective or verb before the audio begins.

## Practice
Complete: The workshop will be held at _____. Predict the answer type, then listen for a location.

## Suggested answer / rationale
place/location
