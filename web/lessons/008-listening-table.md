# BUYE IELTS Module 008: Table Completion

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Compare categories while listening.

## Lesson
Tables often require you to track different people, products, places or time periods. Keep your eyes on the row and column.

## Practice
Make a quick rule for avoiding answers from the wrong row.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Check both row and column before writing.
