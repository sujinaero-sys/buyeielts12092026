# BUYE IELTS Module 144: Subject–Verb Agreement

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Keep the verb aligned with the subject.

## Lesson
Ignore intervening phrases when identifying the grammatical subject.

## Practice
Correct: “The number of students are increasing.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
The number of students is increasing.
