# BUYE IELTS Module 081: Language of Change

**Track:** Writing Task 1  
**Goal:** Vary accurate trend vocabulary.

## Lesson
Rise, increase, climb, grow; fall, decline, drop; remain stable; fluctuate.

## Practice
Choose a suitable verb for a small gradual rise.

## Suggested answer / rationale
edged up / increased gradually.
