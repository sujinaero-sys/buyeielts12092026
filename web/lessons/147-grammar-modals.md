# BUYE IELTS Module 147: Modals for Caution

**Track:** Grammar  
**Goal:** Avoid overclaiming.

## Lesson
May, might, could and would allow careful academic claims.

## Practice
Change “Technology will solve unemployment” to a cautious claim.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Technology may help reduce some unemployment.
