# BUYE IELTS Module 004: Protect Your Spelling Score

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Check letters, plurals and word endings.

## Lesson
Listening answers can be lost through small spelling errors. Pay attention to plural endings, double letters and common suffixes.

## Practice
Write the correct form: accommodation / accomodation.

## Suggested answer / rationale
accommodation
