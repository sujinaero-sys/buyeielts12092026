# BUYE IELTS Module 038: Paragraph Purpose

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Identify why a paragraph exists.

## Lesson
A paragraph may introduce a problem, challenge a belief, present evidence, compare views or propose a response.

## Practice
Label a paragraph that presents an old assumption and then disproves it.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Challenge/correction.
