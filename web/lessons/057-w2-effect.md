# BUYE IELTS Module 057: Explain Consequences

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Show significance.

## Lesson
A consequence paragraph should show who is affected and how.

## Practice
Explain a consequence of air pollution.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Health costs and pressure on healthcare.
