# BUYE IELTS Module 002: Follow the Speaker's Signposts

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Use transition language to anticipate changes.

## Lesson
Words such as however, finally, instead, first and moving on help you follow the speaker's structure. Treat them as navigation signals.

## Practice
Write the likely next focus after: “Now let’s move on to the cost of the project.”

## Suggested answer / rationale
cost
