# BUYE IELTS Module 026: Matching Information

**Track:** Reading  
**Goal:** Locate specific details across paragraphs.

## Lesson
Match the requested information to the paragraph containing the evidence, even when the wording is heavily changed.

## Practice
Find the paragraph that mentions an unexpected research result.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Paragraph containing that result
