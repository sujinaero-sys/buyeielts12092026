# BUYE IELTS Module 006: Form Completion

**Track:** Listening  
**Goal:** Use grammar around a gap to predict the answer.

## Lesson
The words before and after a gap often reveal the grammatical shape of the missing answer.

## Practice
Complete: Participants should bring their _____. Predict the noun type.

## Suggested answer / rationale
equipment / materials
