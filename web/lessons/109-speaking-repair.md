# BUYE IELTS Module 109: Fluency: Self-Correct Smoothly

**Track:** Speaking  
**Goal:** Repair without restarting everything.

## Lesson
Correct briefly and continue. Do not repeat the entire answer.

## Practice
Practise: “He go—sorry, he goes…”

## Suggested answer / rationale
Brief correction + continuation.
