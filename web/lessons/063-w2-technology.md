# BUYE IELTS Module 063: Technology Essay Toolkit

**Track:** Writing Task 2  
**Goal:** Balance innovation with human factors.

## Lesson
Consider access, productivity, privacy, dependency and skills.

## Practice
Develop one balanced point about AI at work.

## Suggested answer / rationale
Productivity gains require reskilling and oversight.
